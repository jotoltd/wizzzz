WEBSITE DESIGN FAQ

How much does a website cost?

It depends on scope. A sharp one-pager costs a lot less than a full platform with booking, payments and admin. The upside of a one-man agency: you're paying for the work, not the office. Tell me what you need and I'll give you a straight quote.

How long does a website take?

Most websites take two to six weeks depending on size and how fast content lands. You'll see progress as it happens, not a big reveal at the end.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Whether it's a refresh or a full rebuild from scratch, I can take your existing site and make it faster, sharper and better at converting visitors.

Do you build e-commerce sites?

Yes — from simple product listings with Stripe checkout to full shop experiences with cart, inventory, and order management. Whatever the setup, it's built to sell.

Will I be able to update the site myself?

If you need to, yes. I can build in a CMS or admin panel so you can update content, add products, manage bookings — whatever makes sense for your business. If you'd rather I handle updates, that works too.

What about hosting and domain?

I handle everything — domain registration, DNS, hosting setup, SSL, deployment. You don't need to think about any of it.
