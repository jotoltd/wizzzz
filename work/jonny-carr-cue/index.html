JONNY CARR CUE

A raffle platform for a custom handmade snooker and pool cue maker. I built the whole thing: ticket purchases with PayPal, user accounts and auth, live draw and winner selection, a product gallery of the cues up for grabs, an about the maker story, session and event bookings, reviews, and contact. Supabase handles auth and data, Resend does the email, all wrapped in a clean, craft-led design with a handwritten display font.
