ADS FAQ

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your goals and your market. I can work with budgets from a few hundred pounds a month upwards. More important than the number is spending it well — I'll tell you honestly if your budget is too small for what you're trying to achieve.

How quickly will I see results?

Paid ads can start delivering traffic within hours of going live. Meaningful performance data usually takes one to two weeks. Optimisation is ongoing — campaigns get better over time as we learn what works.

Do I need a website first?

Usually, yes — ads need somewhere to send people. If you don't have a website yet, I can build one alongside your campaign so everything works together. That's the advantage of having one person do both.

What's your management fee?

I charge a flat monthly fee for campaign management — no percentage of ad spend, no hidden costs. The fee covers strategy, creative, copy, setup, optimisation, and reporting.

Can you take over my existing campaigns?

Yes. I can audit what you're currently running, identify what's wasting money, and either fix it or rebuild from scratch. Either way, you'll know where your budget is going.

READY TO ADVERTISE?

Tell me what you're selling and who you want to reach. I'll come back with a plan.
