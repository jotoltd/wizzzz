WEBSITE + SHOP

THE SLIME STUDIO

A site and online shop for a hands-on slime-making studio in Holt, Norfolk. I built the whole thing: workshop session booking with a live calendar, a full shop with cart and Stripe checkout for DIY kits, handmade slimes and accessories, party bookings, a gallery, and contact. Supabase handles auth and data, Stripe takes the payments, all on Next.js with server-side rendering for fast loads and SEO.
