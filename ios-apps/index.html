iOS APP FAQ

How much does an iOS app cost?

It depends on complexity. A focused utility app costs a lot less than a full social platform with real-time features. Tell me what you need and I'll give you an honest quote — no padding, no surprises.

How long does it take to build an app?

A simple app can be ready in four to six weeks. More complex apps with backend, payments, and integrations typically take two to four months. You'll see real builds along the way.

Do you build Android apps too?

My focus is iOS — Swift and SwiftUI. If you need both platforms, I can advise on cross-platform options, but my strength is building native iOS apps that feel right.

What about the Apple Developer account?

You'll need an Apple Developer account (£79/year). I can help you set it up if you don't have one, or publish under my account if that's simpler for your situation.

Can you add features to my existing app?

Yes — whether it's new features, a redesign, or fixing bugs in an existing Swift codebase, I can pick it up and improve it.
